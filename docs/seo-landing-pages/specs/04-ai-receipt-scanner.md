**Keyword owner:** `/ai-receipt-scanner/`

**Target audience:** Freelancers, self-employed workers, field-service businesses, shop owners, and small operations teams that receive paper or image receipts and need an editable expense record.

**Problem:** Receipt details fade, paper slips disappear, and manual entry separates the purchase evidence from the transaction record. Long or multilingual receipts also make merchant, total, date, tax, category, and line-item review slower.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** Photograph a receipt in the mobile camera, choose an existing image from the gallery, or upload a supported image on the web. Receipt Cycle uses OCR and AI-assisted document understanding to propose the available merchant, total, date, time, payment method, category, currency, tax, subtotal, language, confidence, and line-item details. The user reviews and edits the result before continuing to save the expense.

**Verified product features:**

- Mobile camera capture with flash control and gallery selection.
- Web receipt-image upload for JPG, PNG, and other supported image formats.
- AI and OCR extraction of merchant, total, date, time, payment method, category, currency, subtotal, tax, and line items when visible.
- Multilingual text recognition and detected-language display.
- Document-type classification, confidence indicator, and scan tags for conditions such as partial legibility or missing totals.
- Formatted receipt-text preview.
- Editable review fields before saving.
- Retake option from the review flow.
- Saved transaction can retain receipt image or structured receipt data.
- Search and filtering by merchant, category, date, type, and amount in the transaction workspace.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:**

- No promise of perfect OCR, guaranteed field extraction, or guaranteed line-item accuracy.
- No tax filing, tax deduction determination, tax advice, compliance, reimbursement, or audit guarantee.
- No claim that every language, handwriting style, damaged receipt, or image quality will scan correctly.
- Do not describe AI-inferred categories as final or immutable; users can review and edit them.
- PDF receipt OCR is not a supported receipt-camera claim. PDF statement import is a separate workflow.
- Scanner access can depend on sign-in, subscription eligibility, provider configuration, and administrator availability.
