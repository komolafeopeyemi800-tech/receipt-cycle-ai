**Target audience:** Independent consultants, small-business owners, field-service professionals, small teams attending events or client visits, and operations staff who need a reliable travel-expense record without an enterprise travel platform.

**Problem:** A business trip creates purchases across booking emails, airport counters, hotel desks, restaurants, fuel stops, transit, and cash payments. When receipts, amounts, dates, notes, and payment accounts remain scattered, the traveler must reconstruct the trip later and may still lack the evidence needed for internal review.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** Capture a receipt image or add the expense manually, review merchant, amount, date, category, account, payment method, and notes, then use search and filters to revisit the trip by date range, category, or account. Receipt attachments, editable records, account activity, reports, and CSV export keep the trip connected to the wider business expense history.

**Verified product features:**

- Mobile receipt camera and gallery selection, plus supported receipt-image upload on the web.
- AI and OCR extraction followed by editable review before saving.
- Manual expense entry with merchant, amount, category, date, payment method, account, and notes.
- Custom expense and income categories shared across transactions, budgets, and reports.
- Search by merchant, description, category, or amount.
- Filters for transaction type, category, linked account, start date, end date, and sort order.
- Cash, card, bank, and savings accounts with related account activity.
- Receipt evidence or structured receipt data attached to saved transactions when available.
- Income and expense totals, monthly cash-flow views, and category-aware reporting surfaces.
- CSV export of transaction fields when plan access and administrator settings allow it.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:**

- No dedicated trip object, trip-name tag, itinerary manager, booking system, or travel-policy engine.
- No expense-claim submission, manager approval, automated reimbursement, payroll, or employee repayment workflow.
- No automatic bank or corporate-card feed matching.
- No mileage, per-diem, foreign-exchange conversion, or multi-currency travel calculation claim.
- No tax-deduction determination, tax filing, compliance, or audit guarantee.
- No PDF expense-report export claim. The current verified data export is CSV.
