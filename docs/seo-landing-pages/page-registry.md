This registry is the single source of truth for the initial commercial page set. A secondary phrase may appear naturally on its owner page. It does not create a new URL by itself.

## Priority rationale

The first two pages have the strongest early Search Console association. AI receipt scanning and expense tracking are central product capabilities. The remaining pages map directly to the current sales and finance workspaces. The AI assistant remains last until its commercial intent, shipped behavior, and proof assets are strong enough.

Search Console impressions are prioritization signals only. They are not search volume, demand forecasts, or conversion estimates.

## Ownership rules

- Online invoicing software, small business invoice software, invoice maker software, and invoice tracking software belong to `/invoice-software/` unless future evidence proves a distinct intent.
- Quotation software, estimating software, quote maker, and estimate generator belong to `/estimate-quotation-software/`.
- Invoice payment tracker, business payment tracker, payment receipt software, and accounts receivable tracking concepts belong to `/payment-tracking-software/`.
- Receipt OCR, receipt scanner app, receipt capture software, and receipt organizer concepts belong to `/ai-receipt-scanner/`.
- Business expense tracking software, small business expense tracker, travel expense reporting, and trip expense tracking belong to `/business-travel-expense-tracker/` in the current ten-page plan. Do not create a separate `/business-expense-tracker/` page.
- Travel reconciliation and expense-claim phrases may be addressed on `/business-travel-expense-tracker/` only to explain the records Receipt Cycle prepares. Do not imply automated reconciliation, approval, reimbursement, or claims processing.
- Freelancer and self-employed expense phrases belong to `/freelancer-tax-receipt-tracker/` when the visitor's intent is tax record preparation rather than business travel.
- Tax receipt tracker, self-employed tax tracker, and receipt organizer for taxes belong to `/freelancer-tax-receipt-tracker/`.
- Business budget tracker, small business budgeting app, and expense budget software belong to `/business-budgeting-software/`.
- Customer database, client invoice management, customer billing, and client tracking concepts belong to `/client-management-software/`.
- Expense reporting, business financial reports, spending reports, and expense report management belong to `/expense-reporting-software/`.
- AI finance assistant, AI expense analysis, AI spending analysis, and AI financial insights belong to `/ai-financial-assistant/`.

Do not create separate pages for these variations without new evidence and a completed differentiation check.
