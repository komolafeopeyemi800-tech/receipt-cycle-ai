# Page 2: Estimate & Quotation Software

**Status:** Approved for implementation

**Page name:** Estimate & Quotation Software

**URL:** `/estimate-quotation-software/`

**Search intent:** Commercial investigation and product selection for a tool that creates customer estimates or quotations, tracks their status, and progresses accepted work into invoicing.

**Primary keyword:** estimate software

**Secondary keyword concepts:** quotation software, estimating software, estimate maker, quotation maker, online estimate software, online quotation software, estimate generator, quote generator, quote maker, business estimate software, small business estimating software, contractor estimating software, estimate and invoice software, quote and invoice software, quotation management software.

**Keyword owner:** `/estimate-quotation-software/`

**Target audience:** Freelancers, consultants, service contractors, creative studios, and small service businesses that quote work before invoicing.

**Problem:** A proposal may begin in a document or spreadsheet, but customer details, line items, price changes, validity dates, approval status, and the later invoice become disconnected. Accepted work then has to be entered again.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** Build a structured estimate, preview and share the customer document, track draft, sent, accepted, or expired status, and convert accepted work into an editable invoice draft without rebuilding its commercial details.

**Verified product features:**

- Estimate number, issue date, validity period, and valid-until date.
- Saved customer selection.
- Products or services with descriptions, quantities, and rates.
- Percentage or fixed discount in the mobile workflow; percentage discount in the current web workspace.
- Tax rate and label.
- Notes and terms.
- Estimate document preview and sharing or email handoff.
- Draft, sent, accepted, and expired statuses.
- Duplicate and edit actions in the mobile workflow.
- Customer signature field when enabled in the mobile workflow.
- Accepted estimate conversion to an editable invoice draft.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:**

- Rejected estimate status is not currently implemented.
- No claim of automatic customer approval portals, electronic-signature certification, email-open tracking, automated reminders, recurring estimates, construction takeoffs, material databases, labor costing, project scheduling, tender management, or payment processing.
- Do not imply that a generic quote is a legally binding contract.
