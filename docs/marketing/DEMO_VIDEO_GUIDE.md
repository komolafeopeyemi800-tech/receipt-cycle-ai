# Receipt Cycle Demo Video Guide

A step-by-step guide for recording screen demos of Receipt Cycle on the web app (receiptcycle.com) and the Android app. Follow it in order, read the "Say" lines out loud, and do the "Click" lines on screen. Every menu name below is the real label in the product.

---

## 1. The big idea (say this first)

Receipt Cycle is one place for the money side of a small business. Instead of paying for a receipt scanner, an expense tracker, an invoicing tool, an estimate tool, a payment tracker and a budgeting app, you use one dashboard.

**One-line pitch:** "Scan it, speak it, or upload it. Receipt Cycle reads it, files it, and keeps the proof next to every number."

**Who it is for:** freelancers, contractors, consultants and small businesses. It also helps the accountant or bookkeeper at tax time, because every transaction can link back to its receipt.

**What you get in one dashboard:**

1. Income and expense tracking
2. AI receipt scanner with OCR
3. Document upload (receipts, invoices, bank and card statements)
4. Voice entry (talk and it records the transaction)
5. Manual entry
6. Invoices
7. Estimates (quotes)
8. Payment tracking
9. Budgets
10. Reports and analysis, with filters
11. Export for tax records (Excel, CSV, Google Sheets)
12. Backup, web and Android with the same account

---

## 2. Before you record

Use the checklist so the video looks clean.

- **Make a demo account.** Sign up with a fresh email so you can show the sign-up screen for real. After that, add a few sample records so the screens are not empty. Do not use your own private business data.
- **Use fake business names** such as "Northstar Studio" for customers, and ordinary merchants (a gas station, a coffee shop, an office store) for receipts.
- **Prepare these files on your computer:** one receipt photo (JPG or PNG), one PDF invoice or receipt, one bank or card statement (PDF, CSV or Excel). Keep each under 12 MB for the web.
- **Prepare paper receipts** to scan with the phone. A crumpled one looks real. A flat one scans best.
- **Browser:** use a clean Chrome window, 100 percent zoom, no bookmarks bar, no other tabs. Record at 1920 by 1080.
- **Phone:** turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification pops up. Turn on "Show taps" in Android developer options so viewers see where you tap. Record with the built-in screen recorder.
- **Do not show prices inside the phone app.** On Android, payments are not taken in the app. Show prices only on the website pricing page.
- **Keep sound clean.** Record the voiceover separately if your room is noisy.

**Suggested video length:** a full tour is about 8 to 10 minutes. You can also cut it into short clips (see section 14).

## 3. Part 1: The website tour (home page and pricing)

### 3.1 Home page

**Click:** Open your browser and go to **receiptcycle.com**.

**Say:** "This is Receipt Cycle. It is one app for receipts, expenses, invoices, estimates, payments and budgets, on the web and on Android."

Scroll slowly and pause on each part:

1. **The hero section at the top.** Point at the headline and the two buttons: **Start free** and the Google Play button. Say: "You can start free on the web, or get the Android app from Google Play."
2. **"See Receipt Cycle in action."** This is the interactive demo. Click the tabs one by one: **Capture**, **Transactions**, **Invoices & estimates**, **Budgets**, and **Payments & reminders**. Click a receipt, a bank statement or an invoice to open it larger. Say: "Click any record and you see the original document next to the data we read from it."
3. **"One place for every financial task."** Point at each colored box: Invoicing, Estimates and quotations, AI receipt scanning, Expense tracking, Business budgeting, and Reports and AI analysis. Say a short line for each, using sections 5 to 11 below.
4. **"Made for people who have real work to finish."** Point at the audiences: Freelancers, Consultants, Small businesses and Operations teams.
5. **The FAQ section.** Open one or two questions. Say: "Common questions are answered right here."
6. **The footer.** Point at the product pages (AI receipt scanner, Invoice software, Estimate software, Payment tracking, Business budgeting, Expense reporting, Client management, Freelancer tax tracker, Travel expense tracker, AI financial assistant).

### 3.2 Pricing page

**Click:** **Pricing** in the top menu.

**Say:** "Start free. No credit card is needed for the web app."

Walk through the three boxes:

- **Free ($0):** a 7-day Pro trial and up to 25 transactions.
- **Pro:** a switch at the top changes the middle box between **Monthly ($3.50 a month)** and **Yearly ($35 a year)**. Both start with a 7-day free trial. Click the switch to show both.
- **Pro Lifetime ($60, one time):** the launch offer. Say: "Pay once and keep Pro for life. No monthly charge, no card kept on file."

**Say:** "Pro unlocks unlimited transactions, scanning, voice entry, budgets and exports. One plan covers the web and the phone."

Mention that payments are handled securely by Polar and that you can cancel monthly or yearly plans any time.

## 5. Part 3: Getting records into Receipt Cycle

This is the heart of the video. There are four ways in. The phone has all four. The web has three (upload, voice and manual).

| Way in | Web | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan a paper receipt with the camera | No | Yes |
| Upload a file | Yes | Yes |
| Voice entry | Yes (AI entry) | Yes (AI capture) |
| Type it in by hand | Yes | Yes |

### 5.1 Upload a document (web)

**Click:** **Transactions** in the left menu, then **Add transaction**.

You will see a large box that says **Upload your files**.

**Say:** "Drag a file in, or click browse. You can upload any receipt, invoice, credit card statement or bank statement."

Show the accepted file types printed on the box: **PDF, CSV, XLS, XLSX, JPG or PNG, up to 12 MB.**

Do this on camera:

1. Drag the receipt photo into the box. Wait for it to read. Show the **extracted details** (merchant, date, total, tax, line items) filled in for you.
2. Point at the original document next to the data. Say: "You always see the original beside what we read, so you can check it."
3. Click **Save**. Show the new record in the **Transactions** list.
4. Repeat with a **bank statement** (PDF, CSV or Excel). Say: "A statement can turn into many transactions at once. You review them, fix anything, and save."

**Say:** "The original file is stored with the record. That is your proof at tax time."

### 5.2 Upload a file (Android)

**Tap:** the **+** button in the middle of the bottom bar, then choose the upload option (it opens your phone's file picker).

**Say:** "On the phone you can pick a file from anywhere: your Downloads, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or your email attachments."

The phone accepts any file you pick, and it tells you plainly if a type is not supported. Use a PDF receipt or a statement. Show the review screen and tap save.

### 5.3 Scan a paper receipt (Android only)

**Tap:** the **+** button, then **Scan receipt** (you can also tap **Scan receipt** on the Home screen).

**Say:** "This is the fastest way. Point the camera at a paper receipt."

1. Hold the phone over the receipt. Show the on-screen guide. Say a tip: "Avoid shadows and glare, and keep the whole receipt in view."
2. Tap the round shutter button.
3. Show the processing screen, then the **review** screen with the merchant, date, total, tax and items filled in.
4. Fix anything if needed, then tap save. Show the new transaction in **Transactions**.

**Say:** "Snap, check, save. The photo stays attached to the record."

**Note for the voiceover:** scanning is a phone feature. On the web, you upload the photo instead.

### 5.4 Voice entry (web and Android)

This is for the time you paid for something and got no receipt.

**On the web:** go to **Transactions**, then **Add transaction**, then choose **AI entry**. You can speak or type.

**On Android:** on the **Home** screen use the **Tell AI your transaction** card, choose **Voice**, and tap the microphone. You can also tap **AI capture**.

**Say a sentence out loud,** for example: "I paid forty-five dollars for fuel at Shell yesterday for a client trip."

Show:

1. The words appear as you speak.
2. Receipt Cycle turns it into a record: amount, merchant, date and category.
3. You review it and save.

**Say:** "Talk naturally. It builds the record for you. You stay in control and can edit before saving."

Tip: speak clearly, and say the amount, the place and what it was for.

### 5.5 Add a transaction by hand (web and Android)

**Web:** **Transactions**, **Add transaction**, then the manual entry option (the small pencil or "add manually" control).

**Android:** tap **+** then add a transaction by hand.

Fill in: **Amount**, **Type** (expense or income), **Category**, **Merchant**, **Date**, **Account**, **Payment method**, and **Notes**. Attach a photo or file if you have one. Save.

**Say:** "Prefer typing? That works too. Income and expenses use the same screen."

## 6. Part 4: Working with your transactions

**Click:** **Transactions**.

Show:

- **Search** at the top: type a merchant name.
- **Tabs:** All, Expenses, Income.
- **Filters:** open the filters and narrow by category, date or amount.
- **Open a record:** click one to see the details and the original receipt or file.
- **Edit or delete** from the record.
- **Select several** with the checkboxes. Say: "Select the ones you want and export only those."

**On Android:** open the **Transactions** tab. Show the grouped-by-date list, the search box, the filter button and a tap into a record.

**Say:** "Every record keeps its proof. Click it and the receipt is right there."

## 7. Part 5: Analysis and the new filters

**Click:** **Analysis** in the left menu (on Android, the **Analysis** tab).

Show these parts in order:

1. **Period.** Switch between **Month** and **All time**. Use the arrows to change month.
2. **The three totals:** Expense, Income and Net.
3. **Find receipts.** This is the filter section.
   - Choose a **Category** (for example Groceries).
   - Type part of a **Merchant** name (for example "star" finds Starbucks).
   - Choose **Expenses only** or **Income only** if you like.
   - Say: "Now I only see the receipts that match. The total updates, and the list shows just those receipts."
4. **Export what you filtered.** Under the filters you will see three buttons:
   - **Export receipts (CSV):** one row per receipt.
   - **Export items and prices (CSV):** one row per item bought. Say: "This one is for tracking prices. Filter to Groceries, export, and you can see how the price of milk or eggs changed from one receipt to the next. A column shows the change since you last bought it."
   - **Excel:** the full styled workbook.
5. **Expense distribution.** The pie chart of spending by category.
6. **Income vs Expense** (on the phone).
7. **By category.** Tap a category in the list to filter the receipts to it.
8. **Money leak detection (web) or Entry pattern highlights (Android).** Click **Run AI analysis** or **Generate highlights**. Say: "The AI looks over your saved entries for repeated charges, possible duplicates, or amounts that stand out, so you can double-check them. It is information only, not financial advice."

**Note:** exports are part of the Pro plan. If you use the free trial account, mention that.

## 8. Part 6: Invoices and estimates

Show the **Sales** area. On the web, the left menu has **Sales**, **Invoices**, **Estimates**, **Payments**, **Customers**, and **Items & services**. On Android, tap the **Sales** tab, which has the same sections.

### 8.1 Set up your business first (30 seconds)

**Click:** **More & settings**, then **Business profile**. Add your business name, address, logo and contact details.

Then open **Invoice settings** to set defaults, such as your payment terms and notes.

**Say:** "Set this once. It fills every invoice and estimate for you."

### 8.2 Add a customer and your services

- **Customers:** click **Add customer** and fill in the name and contact details. Say: "Next time, pick them from the list."
- **Items & services:** add what you sell, with a name, description and rate. Say: "Save your regular services so you can add them to a document in one click."

### 8.3 Create an invoice

**Click:** **Invoices**, then **Create invoice**.

Walk through it step by step:

1. Choose the **customer**.
2. Add **line items** (pick saved services or type new ones). Set quantity and rate.
3. Add **tax** and **discount**.
4. Add **notes** and **payment terms**.
5. Open the **Style and color** options. Show:
   - The **template** (a standard layout and a compact layout).
   - The **accent color**.
   - The **paper size** (A4 or US Letter).
   - The switches for what shows on the document (phone number, notes and so on).
6. Open the **preview**. Say: "What you see is what your customer gets. The preview matches the PDF."
7. **Send** it (email or share link) or download the **PDF**.

**Say:** "Your template, color and paper size are saved. The preview, the PDF and the link your customer opens all use the same look."

### 8.4 Create an estimate (quote)

**Click:** **Estimates**, then **Create estimate**.

It works like an invoice: customer, items, totals, style, preview, send. Show the **valid until** date if you set one.

**Say:** "When the customer says yes, convert the estimate into an invoice. The details carry over, so you do not type them again."

Show the **Convert to invoice** action on an accepted estimate.

## 9. Part 7: Tracking payments

**Click:** **Payments** (on Android: **Sales**, then **Payments**).

Show the invoice list with status filters: **All, Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue** (and partially paid).

Do this on camera:

1. Open a sent invoice.
2. Click to **record a payment**. Choose the **amount**, **date** and **method** (cash, bank transfer, card and so on).
3. Record a **partial payment** first. Show the status change to **Partially paid** and the **remaining balance**.
4. Record the rest. Show the status change to **Paid**.
5. Open the **payment receipt**. Say: "Receipt Cycle makes a payment receipt for you, ready to share."

**Say:** "You always know who has paid, who owes you, and how much."

Then open **Reports** and show:

- **Overdue invoices:** everything past its due date, in one list.
- **Cash flow:** money in and money out.

**Reminders (Android):** open **Settings**, then **Notifications**. Show the switches for **Daily Reminder**, **Invoice Reminders** (before an invoice is due) and **Overdue Reminders** (when an invoice becomes overdue). Say: "Turn on the reminders you want. You will only get those."

## 10. Part 8: Budgets

**Click:** **Budgets** (on Android: **More**, then **Budgets**).

Show:

1. **Set a budget:** choose a category (for example Software), enter a monthly limit, and save.
2. **Progress bar:** show how much of the limit is used. Say: "You can see right away if a category is running hot."
3. **Open a budget** to see the transactions behind it, so you know why it moved.
4. Add budgets for two more categories so the screen looks real.

**Say:** "Budgets sit next to your real spending. No separate app, no copying numbers."

Also show the supporting setup screens briefly:

- **Accounts:** your cash, bank or card accounts, with balances.
- **Categories:** your spending and income categories. Say: "Rename them or add your own."

## 11. Part 9: Exporting for tax and your accountant

This is a strong selling point, so give it time.

**Click:** **Backup & export** in the left menu (on Android: **More**, then **Backup & export**).

Show the three options:

1. **Download Excel report (recommended).** A styled workbook. Open it on screen and scroll through:
   - **Summary sheet:** totals, spending and income by category, then by month, by week and by day. These are live formulas.
   - **Every transaction and its document** at the bottom of the same sheet, with a **Download** link beside each transaction that opens the stored receipt.
   - Other sheets: **Transactions**, **Documents**, **Line items**.
2. **Download CSV.** A plain table that opens in any spreadsheet or accounting tool. It includes a link to each document.
3. **Save to Google (Google Sheets).** Sign in with Google and the report is created in your own Google Drive as a Google Sheet. Nothing downloads to your device.

Also show the **sync status** card at the top. It confirms that your records are backed up to your Receipt Cycle account and shows the last sync time.

**You can also export a selection.** On **Transactions**, tick some records, click **Export**, and choose **Selected**. On **Analysis**, filter first, then export just that list.

**Say:** "Your accountant gets a clear workbook, and every number links back to its receipt. That is the proof at tax time. It is the same kind of evidence an auditor would ask for."

**Careful wording:** Receipt Cycle helps you keep tax-ready records. It does not file taxes and it is not tax advice. Do not say it files taxes or guarantees deductions.

**Android backup:** in **Settings**, open **Google Drive backup** to link Drive. Say: "You can also keep a backup copy in your own Google Drive."

## 15. Plain-English glossary for the voiceover

- **OCR:** the app reads the text on a photo of a receipt.
- **Line items:** the individual things on a receipt or invoice.
- **Estimate:** a price quote you send before the work starts.
- **Invoice:** the bill you send after the work.
- **Partial payment:** the customer paid some of the invoice, not all.
- **Overdue:** the due date has passed and the invoice is not fully paid.
- **Evidence or proof:** the original receipt or file stored with the record.
- **CSV:** a simple spreadsheet file that any program can open.

## 16. What to say, and what not to say

**Safe to say:**

- It reads receipts and shows you what it read so you can check.
- Every record can keep its original receipt or file.
- Invoices and estimates use the template, color and paper size you choose.
- You can track partial payments, paid and overdue invoices.
- You can export an Excel report with links back to each receipt, or a CSV, or send it to Google Sheets.
- The same account works on the web and on Android.

**Do not say:**

- That it files your taxes or gives tax or legal advice.
- That it connects to your bank automatically. You can upload statements, but it does not sync a bank feed.
- That it is a full accounting system. It has no general ledger or payroll.
- That scanning works on the web. Web uses upload. Scanning with the camera is on the phone.
- Any price inside the Android app. Show prices on the website only.
- "Unlimited" for the free plan. The free plan has a 7-day trial and up to 25 transactions.
