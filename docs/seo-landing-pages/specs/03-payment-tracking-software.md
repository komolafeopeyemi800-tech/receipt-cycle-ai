# Page 3: Payment Tracking & Receipt Management

**Status:** Approved for implementation

**Page name:** Payment Tracking & Receipt Management

**URL:** `/payment-tracking-software/`

**Search intent:** Commercial investigation and product selection for software that records customer payments against invoices, updates balances, preserves payment history, and produces shareable receipts.

**Primary keyword:** payment tracking software

**Secondary keyword concepts:** invoice payment tracking, payment tracking app, payment management software, business payment tracker, invoice payment tracker, payment receipt software, payment receipt generator, payment receipt maker, payment management system, accounts receivable tracking software, invoice payment management, payment tracking for small business, payment tracking for freelancers.

**Keyword owner:** `/payment-tracking-software/`

**Target audience:** Freelancers, consultants, service businesses, and small teams that need to record customer payments and preserve proof of payment without operating a heavyweight accounting system.

**Problem:** A customer payment may arrive through an existing bank, card, cash, or mobile-money method, while the invoice, balance, payment note, and receipt remain in separate places. Partial payments make the record harder to follow when the amount received is not connected to the original invoice.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** Select an open invoice, record the amount, payment method, receiving account, date, and note, then update the paid amount and remaining balance. Keep the payment in invoice history and generate a receipt with its receipt number, invoice reference, payment reference, method, date, amount, and balance.

**Verified product features:**

- Open-invoice selection before recording payment.
- Full and partial payment support.
- Guard against recording more than the remaining balance in the mobile workflow.
- Payment amount, payment method, receiving account, payment date, and note.
- Generated payment reference and receipt number.
- Invoice paid amount, remaining balance, and paid or partially paid status updates.
- Payment history connected to the invoice.
- Payment confirmation screen.
- Receipt preview with business, customer, invoice, method, account, date, reference, amount, and remaining balance.
- Receipt sharing as PDF or image on supported mobile devices.
- Print or save as PDF and share receipt actions on the web workspace.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:**

- Receipt Cycle does not process card, bank, cash, or mobile-money payments.
- No claim of payment gateways, payment links, automatic collection, automatic bank-feed matching, settlement verification, chargebacks, refunds, automatic reconciliation, or reminder automation.
- Do not imply that recording a payment proves funds were received independently of the user's source record.
- Do not position the page as full enterprise accounts-receivable automation.
