**Keyword owner:** `/business-budgeting-software/`

**Target audience:** Small-business owners, freelancers, solo consultants, service businesses, and small operations teams that want a practical monthly spending plan without adopting a forecasting or enterprise finance platform.

**Problem:** A total monthly target is too broad to guide daily choices. Spending occurs across software, transport, supplies, marketing, workspace, and other categories, while the budget often remains in a separate spreadsheet. By the time a category has exceeded its allowance, the owner must reconstruct which purchases caused it.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** Select a month, assign limits to the expense categories that matter, and compare total budget, recorded spending, and remaining room. Open a category to see monthly progress and the recent transactions behind the amount, then revise the limit when priorities change.

**Verified product features:**

- Month navigation for reviewing or preparing category budgets by period.
- Positive monthly budget limits saved against individual expense categories.
- Summary cards for total budget, spending in budgeted categories, and amount remaining.
- Category rows showing spent, budget, and amount left.
- Over-budget states represented when recorded spending exceeds the category limit.
- Category detail drawer with budget, spent, remaining, percentage used, a monthly progress bar, a simple trend visual, and recent category transactions.
- Ability to edit an existing monthly category limit.
- Spending calculations based on saved expense transactions in the selected month.
- Expense categories that can be managed elsewhere in the product.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:**

- No cash-flow forecasting, scenario planning, profit planning, accounting ledger, or balance-sheet budgeting claim.
- No automatic budget recommendations, autonomous financial planning, or guaranteed savings.
- No budget notification or push-alert claim. The current interface shows progress and over-budget states when the user opens it.
- No bank-feed connection, automatic reconciliation, purchase approval, corporate-card control, or enforced spending limit claim.
- No payroll, inventory, tax filing, multi-entity consolidation, or enterprise expense-policy claim.
- Do not imply that setting a budget prevents a transaction from occurring.
