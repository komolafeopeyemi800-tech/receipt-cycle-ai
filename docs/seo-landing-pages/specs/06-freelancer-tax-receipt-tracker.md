**Keyword owner:** `/freelancer-tax-receipt-tracker/`

**Target audience:** Freelancers, solo consultants, independent creatives, self-employed service professionals, and sole operators who need organized expense evidence for periodic review or tax preparation without adopting a full accounting or tax-filing product.

**Problem:** Receipts arrive throughout the year, while tax preparation happens much later. Paper fades, email confirmations become difficult to find, expense context is forgotten, and a last-minute spreadsheet cannot restore missing evidence. The user needs a repeatable record-keeping habit rather than another annual cleanup project.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** Capture a receipt image or enter an expense manually, review the merchant, amount, date, category, account, payment method, notes, tax, and available line items, then return to the records with search and date, category, and account filters. Receipt evidence and CSV export help the freelancer prepare a cleaner handoff for their own review or an accountant.

**Verified product features:**

- Mobile receipt camera and gallery selection, plus supported receipt-image upload on the web.
- OCR extraction of merchant, total, date, payment method, category, currency, subtotal, tax, formatted receipt text, and line items when visible.
- Editable review before the expense is saved.
- Manual expense and income entry with amount, merchant, category, date, account, payment method, description, and notes where supported.
- Custom categories and category-aware transaction views.
- Search and filters for merchant, description, category, amount, transaction type, linked account, date range, and sort order.
- Cash, card, bank, and savings accounts with account activity.
- Receipt evidence or structured receipt data retained with a transaction when available.
- Income and expense totals, monthly cash-flow views, and category-aware reporting surfaces.
- CSV export when the plan and administrator settings permit it.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:**

- No tax-return preparation, electronic filing, payment, refund, or tax-agency integration.
- No determination that an expense is deductible, compliant, substantiated, audit-proof, or accepted by a tax authority.
- No tax-savings calculation, tax estimate, deduction recommendation, or legal/accounting advice.
- No automatic bank-feed matching or accountant portal claim.
- No promise that receipt images are included inside a CSV export.
- No dedicated tax-year report or tax-form mapping claim.
- Do not use “tax-ready” as a guarantee. Explain that record quality depends on the user's review and professional guidance.
