# Client Management Software Landing Page Specification

Page name: Client & Customer Management for Invoicing
URL: `/client-management-software/`
Search intent: Commercial evaluation of software that keeps customer information, billing documents, balances, and reusable products or services together for a small business.
Primary keyword: client management software for small business
Secondary keyword concepts: customer management software; client management software; customer management system; client management app; customer database software; client tracking software; client invoice management; customer invoice management; client billing software; customer billing software; client management for freelancers; customer management for small business; client invoicing software.
Keyword owner: `/client-management-software/`
Target audience: Freelancers, consultants, service businesses, and small teams that prepare estimates and invoices for repeat customers.
Problem: Customer contact details, billing notes, document status, outstanding amounts, and reusable service prices become scattered across messages, spreadsheets, and old files. That makes routine billing slower and makes it harder to understand the history of one customer.
Receipt Cycle solution: A customer-centered sales workspace that stores customer records, shows connected invoice history and balances, and provides a reusable items and services catalog for estimates and invoices.
Verified product features: Add and edit customers; store contact name, company, email, phone, billing address, status, and notes; search customer records; filter by active or prospect status; view invoice count and calculated outstanding balance per customer; open a customer detail drawer; review linked invoice history, due dates, status, and totals; email a saved customer using the device mail client; create and edit products or services; store item name, description, kind, unit price, category, and active state; search catalog entries; add catalog entries to estimate and invoice line items; reuse descriptions and prices; view estimates, invoices, payments, and sales summaries in their respective connected workspaces.
Unsupported or excluded claims: Full CRM pipeline; lead capture; marketing automation; automated follow-ups; email-open tracking; customer portal; inventory or stock control; job scheduling; project management; contract management; automatic payment collection; bank integrations; automatic reconciliation; recurring billing automation; bookkeeping or accounting suite; shared team permissions beyond what is verified.
Primary CTA: Add your first customer
Secondary CTA: None in the hero.
