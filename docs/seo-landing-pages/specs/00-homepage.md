# Receipt Cycle homepage specification

## Role

The homepage is the broad brand and product-navigation page. It explains the connected Receipt Cycle workspace, establishes the two core jobs of managing spending and getting paid, and routes visitors to specialist commercial pages. It must not compete with a specialist page for its exact primary keyword.

## Audience

- Freelancers and independent consultants
- Small service businesses
- Owners who manage both purchases and customer billing
- Operations or finance administrators who need organized records

## Core promise

Keep the money side of the business connected, from the first receipt or estimate to the final report.

## Hero

- H1: The simpler way to run your business finances
- Support: Keep receipts, expenses, customer work, payments, budgets, and reports in one place. Receipt Cycle helps you stay on top of the details without letting the paperwork take over your day.
- Two hero actions: Start for free for the web app and Download the mobile app for Google Play
- Reassurance: No credit card required. Cancel a paid plan anytime.
- Product proof: original Receipt Cycle interface composition, not a competitor screenshot.

## Information architecture

1. Industry-standard shared header
2. Broad hero and product proof
3. Two connected product paths: money out and money in
4. Outcome cards
5. Detailed product mosaic
6. Connected workflow
7. Audience selector with original illustrations
8. Web and mobile continuity
9. Specialist page directory
10. Objection-handling FAQ
11. Final CTA and shared footer

## Navigation

Primary navigation: Products, Who It’s For, Resources, Pricing, Contact Us. Products is grouped by Sales, Spending, and Insights. Sign in and Start free remain visible actions. Mobile navigation exposes the same hierarchy without hover-only interaction.

## Internal-link ownership

The homepage may describe product capabilities in natural language, but exact specialist clusters remain owned by their registered landing pages. Every commercial capability card links to its canonical owner.

## Copy and claim guardrails

- Original homepage copy only; do not reuse paragraphs from commercial pages or competitors.
- No invented testimonials, star ratings, customer counts, savings claims, or performance statistics.
- No claims for payroll, tax filing, payment processing, inventory, bank connections, or automated recurring invoices.
- No visible em dashes.
- Do not call Receipt Cycle full accounting software.
- Use one H1. The hero may use two distinct actions when they route to the web app and mobile app respectively.

## Conversion strategy

Give a new visitor five-second clarity, show the product early, let visitors self-select by job or audience, answer adoption objections, and repeat the same low-friction CTA at meaningful decision points.
