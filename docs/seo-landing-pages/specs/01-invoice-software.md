**Primary keyword:** invoice software

**Secondary keyword concepts:** invoicing software, online invoice software, small business invoice software, invoice software for freelancers, invoice maker software, invoice tracking software, invoice management software.

**Target audience:** Freelancers, consultants, and service-based small businesses.

**Problem:** Invoices, customer details, line items, due dates, and payment updates become disconnected when they are managed across documents, email, and spreadsheets.

**Receipt Cycle solution:** A connected invoice workspace for creating invoices from saved customer and catalog information, reviewing totals, tracking status and balances, recording payments, and keeping receipts and reports linked to the sales cycle.

**Verified product features:**

- Create invoices with a number, customer, issue date, and due date.
- Add products or services from a saved catalog.
- Set quantities, rates, descriptions, tax, discounts, notes, and terms.
- Preview the finished invoice.
- Save as draft or mark as sent.
- Filter invoices by draft, sent, paid, and overdue status.
- View totals, paid amounts, and remaining balances.
- Record full or partial payments by method.
- View payment history and generate a payment receipt.
- Print or save invoice and receipt views through the browser.
- Connect estimates, invoices, payments, receipts, customers, and reports.

**Unsupported or excluded claims:** Online payment processing, payment gateways, recurring invoices, automated reminders, automatic tax filing, accounting-platform integrations, bank integrations, payroll, inventory management, or multi-currency invoicing.
