# Official competitor source notes

Checked on 2026-10-19. Prices and promotions can change. Confirm the linked official pricing page again before publishing.

The supplied transcript mentions seven third-party products. This six-product set omits Veryfi because it is the least aligned with an end-user small-business receipt-scanner comparison and is positioned primarily around document-processing APIs.

## Zoho Expense

- Product page: https://www.zoho.com/us/expense/
- Pricing: https://www.zoho.com/us/expense/pricing/
- Verified facts: Free is $0 for up to 3 users and includes 20 receipt scans per user per month plus 5 GB of receipt storage. Standard is $4 per user per month billed monthly or $3 billed annually. Premium is $6 monthly or $5 annually. Zoho advertises a 14-day free trial with no credit card.
- Screenshot: `zoho-expense-official.png`

## FreshBooks

- Receipt scanner page: https://www.freshbooks.com/receipt-scanner
- Pricing: https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing?for=freelancers
- Verified facts: Receipt scanning is available on Plus, Premium, and Select, not Lite. FreshBooks advertises a 30-day free trial with no credit card. The US pricing page is promotion- and segment-dependent. The official freelancer pricing result showed list prices of $23 per month for Lite, $43 for Plus, and $70 for Premium, while the captured receipt-scanner page showed a different limited-time discount banner. Use the list price and say promotions vary instead of hard-coding a temporary discount.
- Screenshot: `freshbooks-receipt-scanner-official.png`

## Wave

- Receipts page: https://www.waveapps.com/receipts
- Pricing: https://www.waveapps.com/pricing
- Verified facts: Starter is $0. Unlimited receipt capture is an $8 per month or $72 per year add-on for Starter in the United States and is included in Pro. Pro is $19 per month or $190 per year in the United States. Wave does not advertise a general free trial on these pages. Subscriptions auto-renew and can be canceled.
- Screenshot: `wave-receipts-official.png`

## QuickBooks Online

- Expense tracking page: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accounting/track-expenses/
- Free plan: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/online/free/
- Pricing: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/
- Verified facts: QuickBooks Free is $0 and includes 2 receipt captures per month. The official US pricing page currently shows paid list pricing starting at $38 per month for Simple Start and offers a 30-day free trial on paid plans. Promotions and the plan grid vary by session and locale, so link to the pricing page and avoid publishing a temporary discount as the normal price.
- Screenshot: not captured. The public Intuit page repeatedly returned `ERR_HTTP2_PROTOCOL_ERROR` in headless Chrome. Use the expense tracking page above as the screenshot target in a normal browser.

## Expensify

- Receipt scanner page: https://use.expensify.com/receipt-scanning-app
- Pricing: https://www.expensify.com/pricing
- Pricing detail: https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/billing-and-subscriptions/explore-plans-subscriptions-and-pricing/Understand-Expensify-Pricing
- Verified facts: Submit is free. Individuals can use New Expensify for free with unlimited SmartScans, although some functions, including additional expense categorization, require a paid plan. Collect is $5 per member per month. Control uses custom pricing and is advertised as low as $9 per active member per month under qualifying conditions. Expensify states that a 30-day free trial begins when a paid workspace is created.
- Screenshot: `expensify-receipt-scanning-official.png`

## Shoeboxed

- Features: https://www.shoeboxed.com/features
- Pricing: https://www.shoeboxed.com/pricing/
- Verified facts: Starter is $9 per month or $97 per year. Pro is $29 per month or $297 per year. Plus is $79 per month or $797 per year. Shoeboxed describes its offer as a 30-day risk-free period backed by a money-back guarantee, not a no-charge free trial. A card is charged at signup. The refund condition changes after a Magic Envelope has been shipped.
- Screenshot: `shoeboxed-features-official.png`
