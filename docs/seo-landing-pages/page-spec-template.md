## Approval gate

Do not implement until all of the following are true:

- The intent is distinct from existing pages.
- The primary keyword owner is unambiguous.
- The product genuinely supports the promised workflow.
- Real or purpose-built proof assets are identified.
- Copy, examples, proof, and CTA can be materially different from neighboring pages.
- Substantive headings, paragraphs, examples, comparisons, FAQs, transitions, and metadata can be written without recycling another Receipt Cycle page.
