## Commercial SEO landing pages

Before planning, writing, designing, or coding a commercial landing page, read:

1. `.agents/product-marketing.md`
2. `docs/seo-landing-pages/system.md`
3. `docs/seo-landing-pages/page-registry.md`
4. `docs/seo-landing-pages/keyword-clusters.md`
5. `docs/seo-landing-pages/reference-analysis.md`

Create the page specification from `docs/seo-landing-pages/page-spec-template.md` before implementation. Confirm that the page owns a distinct search intent and does not cannibalize an existing page.

The FreshBooks and Wave screenshots in `docs/seo-landing-pages/references/` are structural references only. Never copy their wording, branded assets, illustrations, screenshots, or exact visual design. Receipt Cycle brand tokens and accurate Receipt Cycle product UI always take precedence.

For visible landing-page copy: do not use em dashes, keyword stuffing, unsupported claims, invented features, fake statistics, fake testimonials, or generic AI marketing filler. Use real product screenshots where available, unique metadata, one H1, accessible semantic markup, responsive layouts, and relevant internal links.
